# Data that could not be uploaded to SLFObservations
Some data used for the training of models, including datasets relating to observations of spotted lanternflies (_L. delicatula_) in the lower 48 United States from 2014 to 2024, could not be uploaded to this repository due to size limitations.
- __Lydemap data__: please follow the directions and download v3.0.3, data only, from ![the official Lydemap repository](https://github.com/ieco-lab/lydemapr) in their ![v3.0.3 directory](https://github.com/ieco-lab/lydemapr/tree/main/download_data/v3.0.3_2024)
