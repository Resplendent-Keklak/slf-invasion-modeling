Data Dictionary for County-Level General Spotted Lanternfly Spread Risk Model
Erica Keklak | 2025-06-25
----
FEATURE COLUMNS:

county: the official full name of the county (data type: string; unit: name)

state: the two-letter abbreviation of the state for the county (data type: string; unit: two-letter abbreviation)

year: the four-digit year of risk evaluation per data point (data type: integer; unit: year)

area: the square mileage of land area in the county (data type: float; unit: square mile)

border_count: the number of counties that share a boundary with each county (data type: integer; unit: counties)

highway_count: number of primary roads lying on the boundary for each county (data type: integer; unit: primary roads)

rail_count: number of train lines lying inside each county (data type: integer; unit: railway lines)

highway_rests_count: total number of all highway rest stops/stations in the county (data type: integer; unit: primary road rest stops)

train_stations_count: total number of all train stations and rail yards in the county (data type: integer; unit: rail stations)

highway_traffic_nymph: average daily traffic of cars and trucks for all daytime hours crossing county boundaries on state highways from April 1 to September 30 (nymph season) (data type: integer; unit: motor vehicles)

highway_traffic_adult: average daily traffic of cars and trucks for all daytime hours crossing county boundaries on state highways from July 1 to November 30 (adult season) (data type: integer; unit: motor vehicles)

highway_traffic_egg: average daily traffic of cars and trucks for all 24 hours of the day crossing county boundaries on state highways from September 1 to June 30 (egg season) (data type: integer; unit: motor vehicles)

rail_traffic_nymph: average daily traffic of rails for all daytime hours crossing county boundaries on state highways from April 1 to September 30 (nymph season) (data type: integer; unit: rail engines)

rail_traffic_adult: average daily traffic of rails for all daytime hours crossing county boundaries on state highways from July 1 to November 30 (adult season) (data type: integer; unit: rail engines)

rail_traffic_egg: average daily traffic of rails for all 24 hours of the day crossing county boundaries on state highways from September 1 to June 30 (egg season) (data type: integer; unit: rail engines)

slf_pop_current: expected or estimated number of individual spotted lanternflies that exist in the county that year (data type: integer; unit: individual spotted lanternflies)

slf_pop_yesteryear: expected or estimated number of individual spotted lanternflies that exist in the county the previous year (data type: integer; unit: individual spotted lanternflies)

expected_nymph_kills: expected or estimated number of times a spotted lanternfly will be killed by a predator in that county that year during nymph season, based on mathematical estimates due to population density of prey, population density of predators, and expected average spotted lanternfly nymphs eaten (data type: integer; unit: individually-killed spotted lanternflies)

expected_adult_kills: expected or estimated number of times a spotted lanternfly will be killed by a predator in that county that year during adult season, based on mathematical estimates due to population density of prey, population density of predators, and expected average spotted lanternfly adults eaten (data type: integer; unit: individually-killed spotted lanternflies)

expected_hosts: expected number of available, known, host plants excluding Ailanthus altissima in the county (data type: integer; unit: individual host plants)

expected_toh: expected number of available Ailanthus altissma plants in the county (data type: integer; unit: individual trees of heaven)

daytime_nymph: average duration of daylight plus half of all twilight available during nymph season (data type: integer; unit: minute)

daytime_adult: average duration of daylight plus half of all twilight available during adult season  (data type: integer; unit: minute)

day_temp_egg: average daytime dry bulb temperature during egg season (data type: float; unit: degrees Fahrenheit)

night_temp_egg: average nighttime dry bulb temperature during egg season (data type: float; unit: degrees Fahrenheit)

coldest_temp_egg: least possible/expected dry bulb temperature during egg season (data type: float; unit: degrees Fahrenheit)

day_temp_nymph: average daytime dry bulb temperature during nymph season (data type: float; unit: degrees Fahrenheit)

night_temp_nymph: average nighttime dry bulb temperature during nymph season (data type: float; unit: degrees Fahrenheit)

day_temp_adult: average daytime dry bulb temperature during adult season (data type: float; unit: degrees Fahrenheit)

night_temp_adult: average nighttime dry bulb temperature during adult season (data type: float; unit: degrees Fahrenheit)

day_hum_egg: average daytime humidity during egg season (data type: float; unit: decimal as percent/relative humidity)

night_hum_egg: average nighttime humidity during egg season (data type: float; unit: decimal as percent/relative humidity)

day_hum_nymph: average daytime humidity during nymph season (data type: float; unit: decimal as percent/relative humidity)

night_hum_nymph: average nighttime humidity during nymph season (data type: float; unit: decimal as percent/relative humidity)

day_hum_adult: average daytime humidity during adult season (data type: float; unit: decimal as percent/relative humidity)

night_hum_adult: average nighttime humidity during egg season (data type: float; unit: decimal as percent/relative humidity)

rain_nymph: average daytime rainfall intensity during nymph season (data type: float; unit: inch)

rain_adult: average daytime rainfall intensity during adult season (data type: float; unit: inch)

wind_nymph: average daytime wind speed during nymph season (data type: float; unit: miles per hour)

wind_adult: average daytime wind speed during adult season (data type: float; unit: miles per hour)

geometry: point-based geometric boundaries per county (data type: geometry; unit: coordinate list)

TARGET COLUMNS:

target: an integer-encoded risk class for each county per year (data type: integer; unit: risk level)
